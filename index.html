What is Carbon Capture Technology?

Carbon Capture and Storage (CCS) is a process that captures carbon dioxide (CO2) from large sources like power plants, preventing it from entering the atmosphere.

The process has 3 main steps:

1. Capture: CO2 is separated from other gases.
2. Transport: It is compressed and transported via pipelines.
3. Storage: It is injected deep underground in rock formations for thousands of years.

3 Main Types of Carbon Capture

1. Pre-Combustion

Fuel is converted into hydrogen and CO2 before burning. CO2 is captured early. Very efficient.

2. Post-Combustion

CO2 is captured from the smoke (flue gas) after fuel is burned. Used in most power plants.

3. Direct Air Capture

Giant fans pull CO2 directly from the open atmosphere. The future of clean air technology.

Benefits of This Technology

1. Fights Climate Change

It can capture up to 90% of CO2, reducing global warming and protecting our planet.

2. Clean Air & Health

Less CO2 and pollution means cleaner air, fewer diseases, and a healthier life.

3. Economic Growth

Creates green jobs, produces useful products like bio-fuel from captured CO2, and supports sustainable industry.
